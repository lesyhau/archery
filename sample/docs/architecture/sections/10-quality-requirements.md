## Quality Scenarios

<!-- archery:help
Contents: Scenarios make quality requirements concrete and decidable (acceptance criteria); keep them specific and measurable. Usage scenarios describe the runtime reaction to a stimulus (incl. performance); change scenarios describe the effect/effort of a modification.

Form - short (Q42): context/background, source/stimulus, metric/acceptance criteria.
Form - long (SEI, Bass et al.): id, name, source, stimulus, environment, artifact, response, response measure.

Edit the "scenarios" list in data/quality-requirements.yaml; link each scenario to the quality goals (1.2) it refines.
